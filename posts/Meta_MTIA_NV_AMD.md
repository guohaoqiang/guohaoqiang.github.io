# AI Accelerators: Meta MTIA, NVIDIA, and AMD

A comparison of modern AI accelerators and their design philosophies.

## Meta MTIA V2 Artemis

Meta's MTIA (Meta Training and Inference Accelerator) [V2](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3695053.3731409) is designed for Meta's specific workloads, including recommendation systems and large language models. The Artemis variant focuses on inference efficiency.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Custom silicon tailored for Meta's infrastructure
- Optimized for inference workloads
- Integration with Meta's existing systems
